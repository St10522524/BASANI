GOT QUESTIONS? WE HAVE ANSWERS!

How long does it take to make a rug?

About 7-12 business days depending on the size and type of design.

Can you turn any image into a rug?

We focus mostly on images, logos, and characters, but most images do work out!

How do I clean my rug?

If you don't have a vacuum, simple soap and a damp cloth work just fine.

Do you offer custom sizes?

Yes! We can create custom rugs in almost any dimension to fit your specific space.

What materials do you use?

We use high-quality, durable yarn that is soft to the touch and built to last.

How much does shipping cost?

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your location and the weight of the rug.

Can I hang my rug on the wall?

Absolutely! Many of our customers use our rugs as unique wall art pieces.

Do you accept international orders?

Yes, we ship worldwide! Please check our shipping page for specific rates.

Are the rugs machine washable?

We recommend spot cleaning only to preserve the quality and texture of the yarn.

How can I track my order?

Once your rug is shipped, we will email you a tracking number so you can follow its progress.
